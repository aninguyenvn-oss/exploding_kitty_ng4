How to Play

1. Setup: Select 2-6 teams and name them.

2. Mystery Boxes: Each round, 3 boxes appear with questions. Teams answer simultaneously in real life.

3. Host picks winner: Click which team answered first.

4. Card Phase: Draw from a 6-card deck (5 point cards + 1 bomb). Keep points or draw again!

5. Bomb: Lose all points accumulated this turn (banked points are safe).

6. Rare Cards: 15% chance for special cards after correct answers!

7. Win: Highest banked score after all 24 questions wins!
